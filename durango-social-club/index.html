The Room

Two Las Vegas chefs, a small dining room, and a menu that changes with the week.

Durango Social Club opened in August 2025 as a stage for chef residencies, pop-ups, and collaborative dinners. In 2026 it became permanent. Dan Krohmer, who owns Other Mama in the same shopping center, cooks alongside Crystina Nguyen, known as Mama, who once led the District One kitchen. The name stayed.

Expect about fifty five seats, low light, and dinner that is meant to run long. The staff will walk you through what is on that night. Come with a group, order across the table, and stay for the egg coffee pie.

What holds it together

The chefs

Krohmer and Nguyen, cooking together every service, not as a one night event.

The room

Small on purpose. Around fifty five seats, warm light, no rush.

The menu

A la carte and built to share. It moves with what the market gives that week.
