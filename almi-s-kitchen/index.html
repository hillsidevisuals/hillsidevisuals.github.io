The menu

A sample of what Almi’s Kitchen plans to serve. Dishes and prices are the owner’s to confirm and will be listed in full in the room and on the ordering app.

To share

Served on one platter over injera, for the table.

  • Vegetarian combination

    Misir wat, kik alicha, gomen, shiro, atkilt and more, arranged together.

  • Meat combination

    Doro wat, key wat and tibs on the same platter, with injera.

  • Kitfo

    Minced beef with nit’ir qibe and mitmita, raw, leb leb, or well done.

Off the fire

Cooked to order on the skillet.

  • Awaze tibs

    Cubed beef seared with onion, jalapeño and awaze.

  • Zilzil tibs

    Strips of beef, quick and hot, rosemary and garlic.

  • Doro wat

    Chicken simmered low in berbere with a hard boiled egg.

All-day breakfast

Served open to close, the way the block likes it.

  • Ful

    Stewed fava beans with tomato, onion, house spice and an egg.

  • Chechebsa

    Torn flatbread tossed in berbere and spiced butter.

  • Enkulal firfir

    Scrambled eggs with onion, tomato and pepper.

Buna

The close. Beans roasted, ground and brewed to order.

  • Traditional coffee service

    Roasted at the table, poured from the jebena, three rounds.

  • Spiced tea

    Black tea with clove, cardamom and cinnamon.

Story

A neighborhood kitchen on Arville, built to feed people well and slowly.

Almi’s Kitchen is opening in the space on Arville St that most recently held an Ethiopian and American restaurant. The room keeps what worked: a warm dining room, a mezzanine, and a patio for long evenings.

The kitchen is from scratch. Berbere and nit’ir qibe are made in house, wats are given the hours they need, and the injera ferments on its own schedule. There is an American plate on the menu too, so nobody at the table gets left out.

What to expect

One platter

Most plates come to share, eaten with injera, no utensils needed.

All day

Breakfast through dinner, so a late lunch or an early dinner both work.

New to it

Tell the server. They will walk you through the platter and pour the first coffee.
