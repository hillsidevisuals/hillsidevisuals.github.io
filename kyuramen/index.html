The room

A room you wander, lit like the middle of the night.

About 5,168 sq ft of honeycomb booths stacked across levels, wrapped around a large sushi bar. A lantern-lined yokocho alley leads past neon fabric curtains to themed private rooms: a ramen shop, an izakaya and a manga cafe. A wishing tree stands near the entrance.

This is the Super concept of the Kyuramen family. The chain opened in New York in 2014 and now runs more than 126 locations. Las Vegas is the second Super Kyuramen in the United States, after Herndon, Virginia.
