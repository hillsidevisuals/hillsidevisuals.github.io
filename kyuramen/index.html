The space

The room

This was a furniture showroom. Now it is 5,200 square feet of honeycomb booths, a full sushi bar, and lantern light across two levels, with a yokocho-inspired alley that ends at a Japanese wishing tree. Each private dining room takes a theme: ramen shop, izakaya, or manga cafe.
