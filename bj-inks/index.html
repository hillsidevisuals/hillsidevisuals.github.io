About.

BJ INKS is a custom tattoo studio in Las Vegas, working privately on South Decatur. Drawing comes first. Every piece starts as linework on paper, worked out before it ever meets skin.

Custom work only. No flash wall, no walk-in lottery. Every appointment begins with a consult so the piece fits the body it lives on.

  1. 01

    Consult first

    Every project starts with a conversation about placement, size, and reference. No guesswork.

  2. 02

    One hand

    You get one artist from sketch to final session. No hand-offs, no rotating chairs.

  3. 03

    Licensed and single-use

    Single-use needles, autoclave sterilized gear, full Southern Nevada Health District compliance.
