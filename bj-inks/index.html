About

The artist

BJ Rascon, tattooing in Las Vegas by appointment.

BJ Rascon is an established Las Vegas tattoo artist, now working independently out of the Tattoo MoA studio on Decatur. The work shown on @bj_inks runs from anime and skulls to fine black linework and wall scale murals.

Drawing comes first. Every piece starts as linework on paper, worked out before it ever meets the skin. Sessions are by appointment, so each piece gets the time it needs.
