A clean-beauty salon in Green Valley

Founded by Ashley Gill in 2025. A team of eight, colour-safe products, and a room designed to feel calm.

Fibre is built around one idea: colour and care that leave the hair in better condition than they found it. The shelf is R+Co, a clean and colour-safe line. Colour is consulted at the chair, because most of it is customised. Scalp Spa treats the scalp the way a facial treats skin. The Blowout Society keeps a standing blowout in the diary for the weeks between appointments.

The room is part of it. Warm daylight, cream tile, white stations, a green-velvet lounge to wait in, and plants. It is quiet on purpose.

Three steps to the chair

New colour clients start with a quick consult. Everything else books straight in.

  1. Consult

    For colour and extensions, a short consult first. In person or a few photos by message. No charge.

  2. Book online

    Pick your stylist and time. A card holds the slot; the deposit comes off the service.

  3. Visit

    Come to Suite 107 on South Eastern. Park at the door. Wait in the lounge.
