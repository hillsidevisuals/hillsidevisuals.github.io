About

Built by someone who has done this before.

A Henderson salon with a clear point of view.

Fibre was founded in 2025 by a salon owner with a long history in the industry and several openings behind her.

The idea is simple: clean, ingredient-aware products, balanced practices, and a room that feels calm. The space also opens up as a venue for small events, ask us.
