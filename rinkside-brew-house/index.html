About

The best view of the ice in Las Vegas

A new brew house taking the restaurant space inside City National Arena in Summerlin.

City National Arena is the Vegas Golden Knights practice facility and one of the busiest community rinks in the valley. Two full size NHL sheets, public skating and learn to skate, youth and adult leagues, tournaments most weekends. The dining room sits between the two rinks, glass on both sides, so you eat and drink with the ice in view.

Rinkside Brew House is a clean, modern take on that room. Rotating local taps, a kitchen worth photographing, full service tables, and a between periods counter for the nights you are on a clock. Not a sports bar. A brew house that happens to sit at the boards.

What to expect

Walk over from the ice

Skate bag friendly, quick counter service when you need it, a real table when you do not.

Local on tap

A draft list that changes often and points at Nevada and the region first.

Built for the whole roster

Beer league teams, hockey parents, Downtown Summerlin regulars, and the kids. Everyone gets fed.
