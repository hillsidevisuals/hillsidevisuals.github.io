About the shop

Where gentlemen still get groomed. A neighborhood barbershop, not a salon and not an experience.

Eddie's Oldschool Barbershop has cut hair around southwest Las Vegas for years, moving from earlier spots on Desert Inn and Durango into one permanent home on South Rainbow at Russell. The idea has not changed: classic barbering, a straight razor, and a chair where stories get told.

Come in for a fade, a scissor cut, a beard shape, or a hot towel shave. No appointment needed, though you can book one. Spanish is spoken. Prices are on the wall so there are no surprises.

Booking

Book a chair online through Squire, or just walk in. Here is how it works.

  1. Pick a time or walk in

    Book on Squire from the button in this site's header, or come by and take the first open chair.

  2. Choose your service

    Fade, scissor cut, beard, or a straight razor shave. Prices are listed on Squire and posted in the shop.

  3. Come to Rainbow and Russell

    5840 South Rainbow Boulevard, Suite 120. Park in the lot, check in, the chair will be ready.
