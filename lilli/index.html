The full menu

Four courses, or eight.

The dining room offers a set menu that follows the season. The Lounge keeps an a la carte list. Prices and courses shift with the market, so the current menu is confirmed when you book.

  1. Four course prix fixe

    The dining room's shorter menu. A seasonal progression from the raw bar through to a sweet.

  2. Eight course tasting

    The full tasting. The kitchen's complete read on the week's ingredients, paired if you like.

  3. The kitchen counter

    Six seats looking straight into the pass. The tasting menu with the cooks talking you through each course.

  4. The Spring Mountain Table

    A private table for up to twelve. A dedicated menu for a celebration or a working dinner.

  5. The wine list

    Old World and New World, recognisable houses alongside smaller estates chosen for character. A full wine by the glass program.

Fifteen seats at the bar and in the lounge, walk-ins welcome. Seasonal small plates, handcrafted cocktails, and the full wine list in a relaxed, intimate room.

Guided by French technique, each dish honours the integrity of every ingredient.

Sixty three seats, in Chinatown.

Lilli's permanent home is in the Green Leaf Lotus complex on Spring Mountain Road. Plaster walls, low pendant light, walnut, a green velvet banquette, and black lattice screens that nod to the street outside.

The dining room

Thirty seats at white linen tables under warm light. The set menu, a considered wine list, and an unhurried pace across two hours.

The kitchen counter

Six seats at the pass for guests who want to watch the tasting menu come together and talk to the cooks.

Group dining and events

The Spring Mountain Table seats up to twelve for a private dinner. Lilli also runs a rotating series of special dinners shaped by collaboration and peak season ingredients.
