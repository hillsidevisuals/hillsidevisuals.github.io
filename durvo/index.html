The name

Durvo means tree.

A Bulgarian root, a Las Vegas table.

Durvo is Bulgarian for tree. Rayna ran a restaurant in Bulgaria before moving to Las Vegas about five years ago; Chef Hanny runs the kitchen.

The name is the menu, Bulgarian comfort cooking grown from plants, and the room is built to match it: dark wood, booth seating, a working bar, and green at every turn. Nothing about it reads like the strip mall it sits in.

What to know

All plant-based

Every plate, all day. Happy that it happens to be vegan.

Breakfast and lunch

Open seven days, roughly 8:30a to 2:30p. Hours TBC.
