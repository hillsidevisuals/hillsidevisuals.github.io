The room

Durvo was built to not feel like a strip mall. It reads as a standalone place, green at every turn.

The main dining room is exposed weathered brick and blackened patinated steel, with warm dark wood tables and grey chairs. Woven rattan pendants hang low, ferns and cascading greenery come down from the ceiling, and a deep green tropical leaf mural runs along one wall. There is a single fresh carnation in a bud vase on every table.

Through the back is a second room that feels like a secret garden at dusk: heavy wood pergola beams overhead, ornate gold crystal chandeliers, a flowering hedge wall, and deep tufted leather banquettes.
