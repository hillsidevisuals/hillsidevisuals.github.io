The name

Durvo means tree

Durvo is Bulgarian for tree. The name is the menu, Bulgarian comfort cooking grown from plants, and the room is built to match it, dark wood, booth seating, a working bar, and green at every turn. It is a neighborhood table, not a strip mall room, open from the first coffee to the last lunch plate.
