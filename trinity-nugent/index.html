Services

A representative menu. Your plan is set at the consultation.

This is a concept list of what a licensed esthetics practice offers. The real menu is built around what Trinity offers and what your skin needs, and is confirmed before you book. Pricing is consultation based, not a fixed price sheet.

  • Consultation and skin reading

    Start here

    A full look at your skin, your history, and your routine, ending with a written plan and a realistic timeline.

  • Signature treatment facial

    Consultation based

    Cleanse, exfoliation, extractions as needed, a treatment mask, and finishing care, chosen for your skin that day.

  • Targeted treatment course

    Planned in a series

    For texture, congestion, or tone that needs more than one visit. Spaced and tracked over several weeks.

  • Brow shaping and tint

    Add on or standalone

    Shape mapped to your face, cleaned up, and tinted to match. Kept natural.

  • Lash lift and tint

    Add on or standalone

    A lift and tint on your own lashes. No extensions, no adhesive weight.

  • Event preparation

    Booked to a date

    A short plan of visits timed to a wedding, a shoot, or a milestone, so your skin peaks on the right day.

The approach

What to expect from a first appointment.

Trinity Nugent is a licensed esthetician. This is a solo practice, so the person who reads your skin at the consultation is the person who treats it at every visit after. That continuity is the point.

A first appointment is unhurried. You talk through what your skin has been doing, what you have tried, and what you actually want. The treatment that day is matched to what is in front of us, not a fixed script, and you leave with a plan in writing.
