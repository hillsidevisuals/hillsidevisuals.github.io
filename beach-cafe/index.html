The cafe

A beach in the middle of the desert, run by people who want you to stay a while.

Beach Cafe is a beach-themed all-day breakfast and lunch spot in southwest Las Vegas, women and family owned and opened in 2007. Recently remodeled, the room is set up to be a place you sit in, with all-day breakfast, a light Hawaiian tilt and American and Mexican plates with what the owners call a Hawaiian zing.

It is meant to be everyone's restaurant. Krystyne Escalambre, one of the co-owners, says the cafe wants to be everyone's friend, everyone's restaurant.

What to expect

  • All-day breakfast

    Scrambles, benedicts, omelets, pancakes, steak and eggs and breakfast tacos, all day.

  • Hawaiian-tilted plates

    Famous fish tacos, the Longbeach BLT and wraps with pineapple or avocado, with a little island seasoning on the side.

  • Everyone's restaurant

    A room built to sit in, with a 10% UNLV student discount for anyone with an ID.
