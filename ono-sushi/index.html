The menu

All you can eat covers the sushi menu at lunch and dinner. A la carte is on the same menu. Everything is made when you order it.

  • Nigiri

    Cut to order at the counter. Salmon nigiri is the one regulars ask for by name.

  • Sashimi

    Salmon, yellowtail, salmon belly, and the day's cuts.

  • Signature rolls

    House rolls, built and cut when you order.

  • Hand rolls

    Rolled to order so the nori stays crisp.

  • Baked & grilled

    Baked mussels, baked scallop, and grilled plates. Baked mussels is the second thing regulars come back for.

  • From the kitchen

    Shrimp tempura, karaage, gyoza, seaweed salad, edamame, miso soup.

  • Dessert

    Mochi ice cream and sorbet.

  • To drink

    Sake, beer, and wine. Happy hour on weekdays, ask for the current times.

Prices are set on the menu in the room and on the ordering apps, and are not listed here.

About Ono Sushi

A neighborhood sushi bar in the Flamingo Eastern retail center, on the southeast side of the Flamingo and Eastern intersection.

The room is small and it is kept clean. There is a sushi counter, a few tables, and a short list of things the kitchen does well. Fish is cut to order rather than held on a belt, which is the reason the all you can eat has a reputation.

Ono Sushi opened on this corner in 2018. New owners took over in 2025 and kept the chef, the menu, and the standard for the fish. The plan is to keep it the same and put a proper face on it.

What to expect

Cut to order

Nigiri, sashimi, and rolls are made when you order them, not stacked ahead.

All you can eat or a la carte

Same menu either way. A la carte if you want two things, all you can eat if you want ten.

A quick weeknight
