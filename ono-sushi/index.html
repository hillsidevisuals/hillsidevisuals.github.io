About Ono Sushi

Ono Sushi has been on the corner of Flamingo Road and Eastern Avenue since 2018, tucked into the Flamingo Eastern center next to Mariana's. New owners took over in 2025. They kept the chef, the menu, and the fish, so regulars still order the salmon nigiri and the baked mussels and get the same plate they always did.

It is a room, not a buffet line. Wood-panelled walls, a white subway-tile counter, rattan pendants, and the tomato-red "Drink Sake Stay Soba" neon. Come in for all you can eat, or sit at the counter and order a few plates a la carte. Reserve ahead for a group or walk in for two.
