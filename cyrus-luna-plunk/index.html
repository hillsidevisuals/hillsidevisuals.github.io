The studio

A small room on Flamingo.

A private one room studio on E Flamingo Rd, set up for headshots and portraits. Natural window light and strobes are both on hand, so a session can go from soft to sharp in the same sitting. Some slots are held open for walk-in booking, with most sessions by appointment.

About

Cyrus Luna Plunk.

Cyrus Luna Plunk is a Las Vegas portrait photographer who builds every session around one question: will this photo get you the callback? The approach is direct and collaborative, and the direction is hands on. Sessions stay simple, the photos are made to be used, and the studio stays small on purpose.
