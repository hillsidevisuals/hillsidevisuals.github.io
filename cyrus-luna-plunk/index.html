Studio

A private room on East Flamingo.

Where the sessions happen.

The studio is a small suite at 1641 E Flamingo Rd, a street-level commercial building near Maryland Pkwy, walkable and mid-Strip-adjacent.

Private, controlled light, no rush. You book a time, you get the room, and we shoot until the photo is right.

About

Studio light for Las Vegas artists.

Who is behind the camera.

Cyrus Luna Plunk photographs the people who live or die by their headshot: actors, musicians, dancers, models, and the wider Las Vegas creative bench.

The work is clean, current, and lit like a gallery print, never a webcam grab. The point is the outcome: the callback, the booking, the look that travels.
