About Lucky House

A bright, busy Cantonese seafood and dim sum room for the southwest valley.

Lucky House opened on South Durango in early 2024. The idea was simple. The southwest valley is a long way from the Spring Mountain dim sum corridor, and a good neighborhood room could save everyone the drive and the parking.

Expect a full dining room most weekends, round tables built for groups, dim sum ordered off a cart and cooked to order, and a roast counter working through duck and pork belly all day. Service is quick. Beer and wine are now served in the room.
