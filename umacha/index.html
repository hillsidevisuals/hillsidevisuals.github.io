About ÜMACHA

ÜMACHA does cheese cream and ceremonial matcha. We top our teas with a smooth, silky cheese cream foam and grind ceremonial grade matcha into crisp, grassy lattes. It is soft, juicy, and a little bit bouncy, the way a good drink should be.

The Blue Diamond store is our newest location, the first in Southwest Las Vegas. We are happy to be pouring here.

M

Ceremonial grade matcha

Rich, grassy, and balanced. The heart of our Kyoto Matcha line.

C

Signature cheese cream

A silky foam that sits on top of the tea. Drink it through the crema or stir it in.
