About Taco Boom

A counter-service taqueria on South Jones Boulevard, west side of Las Vegas.

The order is simple. Pick your fillings, build your plate at the self-serve salsa bar, and eat. Carne asada and tripa are what regulars come back for. Capeados, battered fish and shrimp done Mexican street style, are the thing to try if you have not.

It is a small, new spot in a strip-mall bay with a shared lot. Fast tacos, big flavor, no dine-in fuss. Walk in, call your order ahead, or order on Uber Eats for delivery across the west side.

How to order

Three ways to get Taco Boom. All of them are quick.

  1. Order at the counter

    Walk in, read the board, and build your order. Cash and cards.

  2. Call ahead

    Phone your order in and skip the wait. Call or text (702) 466-2871.

  3. Get it delivered

    Order on Uber Eats for delivery across the west side of Las Vegas.
